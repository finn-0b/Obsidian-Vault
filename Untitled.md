# Phase 1 Checklist — Weeks 1-4

**Tools:** Kwiziq (grammar) · Anki (vocab) · French-dubbed anime + French subs (listening) · Graded reader (reading) **Daily target:** ~2-2.5 hrs

## Setup (before Week 1 — do once)

- [x] Take Kwiziq's placement test so it starts you at the right level
- [ ] Install Anki, download a French 5000-word frequency deck, set new-card limit to 25-30/day
- [ ] Pick your anime: choose something with **simple, everyday dialogue** to start — slice-of-life is easier to follow than action/fantasy exposition. Save something seinen-adjacent for later once vocab builds up.
- [ ] Get a graded reader — _Le Petit Prince_ or a LingQ beginner text

## A note on subs

Use **French subtitles**, not English — English subs let your brain skip the French entirely. If an episode is too fast to follow, watch it once with subs, then again without pausing, then a third time pausing to look up 2-3 phrases you keep missing. Don't look up everything; that kills momentum.

## End of Phase 1 — Self-check before moving to Phase 2

- [ ] Anki: reviews are a steady daily habit, deck sits around 500-700 words learned
- [ ] Grammar: present, passé composé, imparfait, futur proche all feel usable (not perfect — usable)
- [ ] Listening: French subs feel readable at normal speed for simple dialogue, even if some lines still need a pause
- [ ] Reading: you've finished (or nearly finished) your first graded reader
- [ ] **Best sign of readiness:** rewatch your very first Week 1 episode — if it feels noticeably easier, you're on track for Phase 2

If most boxes aren't checked, that's fine — repeat the weakest week rather than pushing forward on shaky grammar, since Phase 2 builds directly on passé composé/imparfait.
